## Research

**How fragile a deep lung-nodule detector appears depends on the texture of simulated noise: a paired evaluation of training-time augmentation**
M. S. Yaseen, A. Okatan · manuscript, 2026
Paired evaluation of dose and blur augmentation for a 3D RetinaNet detector on LUNA16, with projection-domain noise calibrated on TCIA low-dose CT and pre-specified statistics.
[Code](https://github.com/Mahmoudyasen/lung-nodule-detector-robustness) · [Pre-registered analysis plans](https://github.com/Mahmoudyasen/lung-nodule-detector-analysis-plans)
