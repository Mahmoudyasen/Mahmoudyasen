## Research

**A Hybrid Deep Learning Approach for the Early Detection of Lung Cancer: A Comparative Study on the NLST Dataset with a Focus on Reducing False Positives**
M. S. Yaseen · M.Sc. thesis, Istanbul Aydın University, to be submitted March 2027 (advisor: Prof. Dr. Ali Okatan)
Two-stage CT system: a 3D RetinaNet detector, then a multi-scale false-positive-reduction stage whose score is fused with the detector's score.
- On LUNA16 (CPM 0.931) it needs 40 % fewer false positives per scan at 90 % sensitivity than the detector alone.
- It was then evaluated, with pre-specified analyses, on 72,538 NLST screening exams against the trial radiologists.

[Code](https://github.com/Mahmoudyasen/Thesis-Public-Repository)

**At equal level, simulated noise texture strongly affects how fragile a deep lung-nodule detector appears, with or without training-time augmentation**
M. S. Yaseen, A. Okatan · manuscript, 2026
Paired evaluation of dose and blur augmentation for a 3D RetinaNet detector on LUNA16, with projection-domain noise calibrated on TCIA low-dose CT and pre-specified statistics.
[Code](https://github.com/Mahmoudyasen/lung-nodule-detector-robustness) · [Pre-registered analysis plans](https://github.com/Mahmoudyasen/lung-nodule-detector-analysis-plans)

**Machine Learning Techniques to Predict Total Precipitation Over Specific Seasons**
O. Sawalha, M. Yaseen, Z. Aslan · 12th International Conference on Image Processing, Wavelet and Applications (IWW2025), Istanbul Technical University, 3–5 November 2025 · extended abstract in the IWW 2025 Book of Extended Abstracts (İTÜ Yayınevi, 2026, p. 123)
Seasonal total-precipitation prediction over Istanbul from ERA5-Land and MODIS water vapour with Random Forest and Gradient Boosting.
[Code](https://github.com/Mahmoudyasen/precipitation-ml-istanbul) · [Conference program](https://iww.itu.edu.tr/program/program) · [Book of Extended Abstracts](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/404975541_IWW_2025_Book_of_Extended_Abstracts)
